# Book 1 Timeline — 298 AC

Month-by-month spine of *A Game of Thrones*, from Jon Arryn's death to Robb Stark's crowning.
Roughly seven months. Dates in ASOIAF are deliberately loose; the month numbering below is a
**production calendar** — a consistent internal scaffold for pacing, save timestamps, and act
cards — not a claim about canon precision.

> **A note on seasons.** The design brief describes Chapter 1 as running "Spring to Autumn." In
> canon, *A Game of Thrones* takes place at the tail end of a nine-year **summer**; the Conclave
> does not declare autumn until the opening of *A Clash of Kings*. The game keeps that canon fact
> (characters talk about the long summer ending, and "winter is coming" lands because it is still
> warm), while the **art direction** follows the spring→autumn palette arc the brief asks for:
> Act 1 is green and gold, Act 5 is rust, mud, and low grey light. Seasonal *feeling* travels;
> the in-world season does not.

invisible — which is a skill, and she knows it.

## Excluded from Chapter 1

Deliberately out of frame, to keep the scope to three location clusters:

- **Everything in Essos.** Daenerys, Drogo, Viserys, the dragon eggs.
- **The Wall.** Jon Snow's storyline entire.
- **The Eyrie.** Catelyn, Tyrion, Lysa, the sky cells, the trial by combat.
- **The Trident, the Kingsroad, Darry.** Referenced by raven and rumour only.
- **The Green Fork.** Fought by the half of the army Torren is not in — which is the point.

Each of these is a candidate for Chapter 2, and none of them needs to be built now.
