# Chapter 1 — Perspective Map

Which protagonist witnesses which canonical event, and *how close they stand to it*.

The governing rule: **no secondhand accounts of anything the chapter is about.** If an event is on
the act's spine, the player's eyes are on it. Events reach the player by rumour only when the
distance is itself the point (Torren cannot know what is happening in King's Landing; that
helplessness is Act 5's emotional engine).

Proximity grades:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **EYES** | On screen, in the room, unobstructed. Player controls a character who can see it happen. |
| **EDGE** | On screen but partial — through a door, from a crowd, from the back of a hall. Player hears fragments. |
| **AFTER** | Player arrives immediately after. Sees the consequence, not the act. |
| **WORD** | Reported by another character. Used sparingly and never for an act's spine event. |

## Act 1 — *The Fall* · Ser Torren · Winterfell

Twelve scenes, two of them free-roam. The act's shape is: an ordinary castle, then an
extraordinary one, then a broken one.

| # | Scene | Canonical event | Grade | How it is staged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning Duties | Jon Arryn's death; Robert rides north | **WORD** | Correct use of WORD — it is Act 2's spine, not Act 1's. Movement/interact tutorial. Torren states his want in the first three minutes. |
| 2 | Preparing for a King | — | — | Free-roam. Learn the castle *before* it fills with strangers. Rodrik, Jory, Hodor, a stable boy. The honour guard is optional and is the act's first pure piece of ambition. |
| 3 | A Deserter's Head | Execution of Gared by Eddard Stark | **EYES** | Torren stands in the ring. The camera holds on Bran, never the block. |
| 4 | Six Pups and a Seventh | The direwolf and her pups | **EYES** | Free-roam of a stretch of road. The albino is off the path and only found by wandering. |
| 5 | The King Comes North | Robert's arrival | **EYES** | Torren pinned in the honour guard. Nyra is on screen in the baggage train and is *not* flagged. |
| 6 | Torchlight | Robert at Lyanna's tomb | **EDGE** | Torren holds a torch at the stair. Two optional steps closer, each a clearer fragment. |
| 7 | The Welcoming Feast | The feast | **EYES** | Free-roam of the hall. **Brief Encounter #1.** Benjen, Jon and Tyrion sighted across the room. |
| 8 | Days of Feasting | The court settles in | — | Free-roam over several days. Robb (spar), Jon (the Wall), Tyrion (decent to him, which complicates Act 5), Benjen (the truest thing anyone says in the act). |
| 9 | The Broken Tower | **Bran's fall** | **EYES** | An ordinary errand across an ordinary yard. Optional look-up. A shuttered window, and nothing else, ever. Torren reaches him first. |

| 12 | Winter Is Coming | — | — | The rampart. Robb alone in the yard below. The three words are never spoken by a character; the act is named for them and they are left in the player's mouth. |

**Act 1 exit flags:** `act1_bran_fell`, `act1_torren_carried_bran`, `act1_encounter_feast`,
`act1_encounter_departure`, `act1_complete`, plus trait totals and five relationships.

## Act 2 — *The Investigation* · Nyra · The Red Keep

Nyra has no authority and no access. Her entire toolkit is **being where nobody looks** — which is
why almost everything here is EDGE, and why that is not a compromise. Act 2 is a game about
fragments, and the player assembles the truth before Ned does.

| Canonical event | Grade | How it is staged |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival of the court in King's Landing | **EYES** | Establishing free-roam of the Red Keep. Learn the service corridors — the act's real map. |
| Ned's first small council | **EDGE** | Nyra pours. She is in the room and not in the room. Player chooses where to stand; different positions catch different lines. |
| Ned questions Pycelle | **EDGE** | Through a serving hatch while carrying a tray. Timed: linger too long and she is seen. |
| Jon Arryn's last errands (armourer, Gendry) | **EYES** | Nyra is sent to Tobho Mott's on a household errand and crosses Ned's path. Gendry is in the yard. Black hair, blue eyes. The player is shown, not told. |
| Ser Hugh of the Vale dies in the lists | **EYES** | From the household stand. The lance goes through his throat. |
| The Tourney of the Hand | **EYES** | Loras and Gregor; the Hound stops his brother. Free-roam of the tourney grounds between bouts. |
| **The ledger of Robert's bastards** | **EYES** | Act 2's turning point. She is cleaning Jon Arryn's old solar. She can read — barely, and that is a secret too. Every name, black of hair. |
| Cersei and Littlefinger, separately, take her measure | **EYES** | Two conversations. Both are threats. Neither sounds like one. |
| Jaime's men attack Ned in the street | **AFTER** | Nyra is at the market. She comes back through the blood. Jory Cassel's body — a character the player *met in Act 1, as Torren*. |
| Ned resigns the Handship | **EDGE** | She is pouring the wine when the pin hits the table. |
| Ned and Cersei in the godswood | **EDGE** | Behind the heart tree. The most dangerous knowledge in the capital, acquired by accident. |
| Robert gored by the boar | **AFTER** | He is carried past her, and he is enormous, and he is dying. |
| **Robert's death** | **EDGE** | Outside the door of the royal bedchamber. She hears the will dictated. |

**Act 2 exit flags:** `act2_read_ledger`, `act2_saw_gendry`, `act2_heard_godswood`,
`act2_robert_died`, `act2_cersei_noticed_nyra`, `act2_littlefinger_noticed_nyra`.

The last two matter enormously in Act 4 and in later chapters. Being noticed is Nyra's version of
taking damage.

## Act 4 — *The Traitor's Daughter* · Nyra · King's Landing

Act 4 is the shortest and the worst. It is a horror act structurally: the player's toolkit
(invisibility) stops working, because the Red Keep is now hunting people.

| Canonical event | Grade | How it is staged |
|---|---|---|
| Ned moves on Joffrey's claim; the gold cloaks turn | **EYES** | Nyra is behind a pillar in the throne room. Littlefinger's knife at Ned's throat. |
| Ned's arrest | **EYES** | |
| The Stark household is killed | **EDGE / AFTER** | Corridors she has walked all game, now with bodies in them. No combat. She hides. Detection, not HP. |
| Syrio Forel's last stand | **AFTER** | A wooden sword and a room she cannot enter. |
| **Arya escapes** | **EYES** | A girl runs past her with a blade. A gold cloak asks Nyra which way she went. **Major player choice: lie, tell, or say nothing.** Sets `act4_lied_for_arya`. |
| Sansa made to plead for Ned | **EDGE** | From the gallery. |
| The city turns: riots, closed gates, fear | **EYES** | Free-roam of a King's Landing that has become hostile. |
| Nyra is questioned | **EYES** | By whoever noticed her in Act 2 — Cersei, Littlefinger, or (if neither) Ser Meryn. Three different scenes. **This is where Act 2's flags pay off.** |
| **Ned's confession at the Great Sept of Baelor** | **EYES** | In the crowd. She is pushed forward, not by choice. |
| **The execution** | **EYES** | Ilyn Payne. Ice. The player may hold the look button or release it. Either is recorded. `act4_watched_execution`. |

**Act 4 exit flags:** `act4_ned_executed`, `act4_watched_execution`, `act4_lied_for_arya`,
`act4_interrogated_by_<who>`, `act4_nyra_survives`.

## The Brief Encounter — Staging Notes

Two beats, both in Act 1, both under thirty seconds, neither with a line of dialogue between them.

**Encounter #1 — The Feast.** Torren is free-roaming the Great Hall. Nyra crosses on a fixed path
carrying a wine jug. If the player's facing intersects her path, the camera does *nothing special*
— no push-in, no music sting. One held frame of eye contact, then she is past. Players who never
look at her still get the flag; players who follow her get one extra beat of her looking back.
`act1_encounter_feast`.

**Encounter #2 — The Departure.** Scripted. She drops a trunk in the yard; Torren catches it. The
sprites' hands overlap for four frames. She says nothing. He says nothing. If the player mashes
interact, he says something and it is wrong, and that is recorded too (`act1_torren_spoke_first`).
`act1_encounter_departure`.

**Rules for the whole arc.** Neither learns the other's name in Chapter 1. Neither thinks about the
other on screen. No music theme is shared between them yet — the shared motif is introduced in
Chapter 2, and it works *because* it was withheld. The romance is a Chapter 3+ payoff; Chapter 1
plants two seeds and walks away.

## Dramatic Irony Ledger

The alternating structure exists to give the player knowledge the characters lack. Tracked
deliberately:

| The player knows | Because | The character who doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| Bran was pushed | Act 1 staging | Everyone. Forever. Torren has one closed shutter and never gets further. |
| Joffrey is not Robert's son | Act 2, the ledger | Torren, the entire North |
| Ned is already dead | Act 4 | Robb's army, for one whole act of Act 5 |
| Jory Cassel is a person | Act 1, as Torren | Nyra, who steps over his body in Act 2 |
| Arya is alive and loose in the city | Act 4 | Robb, Catelyn, Torren |

The last one is the hook into Chapter 2.
